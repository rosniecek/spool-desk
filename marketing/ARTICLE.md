# SPOOL: Every story has a thread

A token name can be copied. A ticker can be reused. A confident post can travel farther than the facts behind it.

SPOOL starts somewhere more useful: the mint address.

Built for people exploring Solana tokens, SPOOL is a small investigation desk that turns a public mint address into a readable, source-linked report. Paste an address, pull the thread, and see what the available evidence actually says.

## Open the file

A SPOOL inspection makes two independent source requests. Solana mainnet provides finalized mint-account information. DEX Screener provides reported markets for that token.

The mint side shows whether mint and freeze authorities remain active, the token program, decimal precision and the observed slot. The market side organizes up to 30 matching base-token pairs by reported liquidity, with indicative prices, volume and direct links to the market sources.

There is no wallet connection, signature or purchase required. The inspection cannot move your funds.

## Keep the loose ends visible

A report is useful only when it tells you what is missing.

If a provider is unavailable, SPOOL shows a partial report. If no matching markets are returned, it says that no markets were observed. Neither outcome is rewritten into a reassuring score.

Even a complete report is not a safety verdict. Revoked mint authority does not establish that a token is safe. Reported liquidity does not prove liquidity is locked or that a trade will execute. Additional Token-2022 controls can matter, and SPOOL includes raw extension data without pretending to provide a full policy audit.

The desk does not perform holder-concentration analysis, creator investigations, honeypot detection or trading simulations.

## Take the receipt

Every report can be exported as JSON. It includes the address, retrieval timestamps, source observations, limitations and a SHA-256 checksum of the report body. That checksum identifies the file contents; it is not an independent signature or certification.

The last twelve reports remain in your browser, making it easy to return to a previous file or request a fresh observation. This history is local to your browser, not a shared public activity feed.

## A little character. A clear purpose.

SPOOL lives in a pixel-art workroom, guided by Bobbin, a small investigator following an orange thread through a paper-filled desk. The opening animation belongs to that world. The product stays one action away.

The prospective SPOOL token represents the project's community identity. It is not required to use the tool, and no revenue rights, automatic fees or buybacks are implemented. The utility stands on its own.

Start with an address. Follow the evidence. Keep the receipt.
